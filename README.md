#   ¯\_(ツ)_/¯

Systems, intelligence, security, sound, and play.

This GitHub is a living laboratory for simulations, prototypes, strange tools, generative sound, hardware curiosities, games, and unfinished fragments.

I am interested in how complex systems become understandable, playable, audible, and useful.

## Main thread

### `systems-and-intelligence`

Experiments, simulations, and reflections on emergence, self-organization, artificial life, multi-agent systems, and the nature of intelligence.

This is currently the main repository and the conceptual center of gravity.

## Other threads

### `the-weaving-void`

An expanding grimoire of esoteric programming languages, poetic computation, strange logic, and bizarre computational paradigms.

### `the-weaving-sound`

Generative audio experiments and SuperCollider sketches.  
A place for procedural atmosphere, sonic systems, and sound as another way of thinking.

### Flipper Zero / field systems

Small experiments around signals, devices, and playful hardware:

- `ble-density-gauge` — a passive BLE scanner for the Flipper Zero that turns invisible Bluetooth advertisements into a real-time index of the surroundings
- `flipper-zero-pinball` — a playable pinball arcade for the Flipper Zero, because you obviously need a flipper for your Flipper

### Small games & fragments

- `arch-pacman-clicker` — `[pacman -S]... but fun.`
- `frnkptrln.github.io` — curated fragments, project notes, and surface representations

## Current / next

`zero-day-genesis` is still local: an early Godot prototype for a cybersecurity learning game about emergent risk, trust, vulnerabilities, and defense systems.

It is not about exploit tooling.  
It is about making cyber risk playable, visual, and systemic instead of static training material.
